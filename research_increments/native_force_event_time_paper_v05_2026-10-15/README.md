# Event-timed robot policy research draft v05

This bundle contains the complete ICML-format research draft **Failure-Preserving Experience for Event-Timed Robot Policies: A Causal Contract and a Predeclared Learning Study**, with current TeX, Markdown, ten primary-source references and code-native vector figures. It is an evidence-bound research draft, not a submission or an accepted method paper.

**Scientific status: MAJOR_OPEN.** The main VLA comparison, actual student OPD rollouts and qualified scientific S/F/R data remain unexecuted. One P0 CPU engineering checkpoint exists; it is not a language-conditioned VLA or a heldout-task result. M0/M1/M2 are three closed diagnostic attempts with distinct timeout/complete/censored outcomes. M1's omitted scalar remains unrecoverable; M2 preserves a new signed raw sample without changing the legacy rejection or qualifying physical loads. G1 has scoped software conformance and a retained synthetic 13,995-command trace, with no native installation, real student or teacher model call. Real-hardware and chemical outcomes are absent.

The contract separates physical occurrence from confirmation/audit/delivery, preserves original deadline and absorbing known hard failure, and distinguishes complete S, confirmed failed-student correction F, and independently executed local R. Unknown original outcome can allow an authorized safety abort without becoming F/S/R. Shared frozen training/state-matched offline or non-actuating shadow checks bind byte-identical finite public prefixes. Independent closed-loop M/B3 trials pair initial world cells/seeds and match interfaces/modalities/history capacity/delivery and latency protocols/teacher/guards/data/budgets, while allowing action-induced observation divergence. Each arm retains its own actual inference-start prefix; copying another arm's future trajectory is prohibited. Conditional MI=0 is per eligible input, not equality of closed-loop histories. The ledger derives only from its own eligible delivered prefix; no older discarded memory or private teacher information enters its policy input.

The unexecuted study preserves S512 ancestry and a union of at most 1,024 unique state/target pairs. It fixes 5,000 ancestor plus 10,000 adaptation updates; collector validation does not select among 21 learned-run equivalents. Half of the proposed 12 evaluation cells are irreversible negatives, so pooled complete success has a 50% ceiling; pooled 0.15 improvement implies 0.30 in the recoverable half. Task-graph sources and physical feasibility still require qualification.
